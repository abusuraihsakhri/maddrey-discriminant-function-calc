Results and interpretation

Enter measurements and select “Calculate scores” to see the result.

Clinical decision support only Scores do not establish a diagnosis or authorize corticosteroid treatment. Confirm clinical findings and consider MELD, infection, gastrointestinal bleeding, renal failure and other contraindications. The 2024 ACG guideline emphasizes MELD >20 when assessing severe alcohol-associated hepatitis.

Calculations

mDF = 4.6 × (patient PT − control PT) + bilirubin (mg/dL)

ABIC = 0.1 × age + 0.08 × bilirubin + 0.8 × INR + 0.3 × creatinine

ABIC categories: low <6.71; intermediate 6.71–<9.0; high ≥9.0. Historical MELD uses the logarithmic three-variable formula; scores are not MELD 3.0.

Privacy: calculation runs in your browser. The application does not transmit, save or log entered measurements. No patient identifiers are needed.
